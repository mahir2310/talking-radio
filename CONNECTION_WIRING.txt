ESP8266 TALKING RADIO - EXACT CONNECTION WIRING
================================================

This wiring is based on the actual boards in your photos:

  1. NodeMCU ESP8266 Lua board
  2. Purple GY-PCM5102 DAC board
  3. Green PAM8403 amplifier with volume knob
  4. One speaker
  5. 1000 uF / 10 V electrolytic capacitor
  6. Loose three-pin IR receiver


IMPORTANT BEFORE STARTING
-------------------------

1. Disconnect USB and all power while making or changing wires.

2. For the first test, use a good 5 V USB adapter rated for at least 2 A.

3. Do not connect any part of this circuit to the old radio's mains wiring.

4. The PAM8403 speaker outputs are bridge outputs. Neither speaker wire may
   connect to GND.

5. Do not connect the PAM8403 right and left outputs together.

6. The purple PCM5102 pin marked A3V3/3V3 is an onboard 3.3 V regulator
   output. When PCM5102 VIN is powered from 5 V, leave A3V3 unconnected.

7. The IR receiver model number is not readable in the supplied photo.
   Do not connect it until its pin order is confirmed.


PART A - NODEMCU TO PCM5102 DIGITAL AUDIO WIRES
-----------------------------------------------

NodeMCU pin              Connect to PCM5102 pin
----------------------   -------------------------
D4 (GPIO2)               LCK
D8 (GPIO15)              BCK
RX (GPIO3)               DIN
G (any GND pin)          GND
VU (USB 5 V)             VIN
G (any GND pin)          SCK
3V (3.3 V pin)           XSMT
G (any GND pin)          FLT
G (any GND pin)          DEMP
G (any GND pin)          FMT

PCM5102 pins that remain unconnected:

  A3V3 / 3V3
  ROUT
  the AGND beside ROUT
  the 3.5 mm headphone/line socket

The PCM5102 has two AGND pins. Use the AGND beside the top analog output marked
LOUT/LROUT for the amplifier audio-ground connection described in Part B.

Notes:

  - Use the printed labels on the PCM5102 board. Do not count positions from
    the photograph.
  - SCK is not the same pin as BCK. SCK goes to GND; BCK goes to NodeMCU D8.
  - RX is used as digital audio output. This is correct for ESP8266 I2S.


PART B - PCM5102 TO PAM8403 AUDIO INPUT
---------------------------------------

On your PAM8403 board, the three input holes beside the top of the right edge
are marked R, G and L.

PCM5102 pin              Connect to PAM8403 pin
----------------------   -------------------------
LOUT/LROUT               L (audio input)
AGND beside LOUT/LROUT   G (audio input ground)

Leave the PAM8403 input marked R unconnected because only the left channel and
one speaker are being used.

Use short wires for LOUT/LROUT and AGND. If possible, twist these two wires
together.


PART C - PAM8403 POWER
----------------------

Your PAM8403 has a two-hole POWER group marked + and -.

NodeMCU pin              Connect to PAM8403
----------------------   -------------------------
VU (USB 5 V)             POWER +
G (any GND pin)          POWER -

This VU method is for a first low-volume test using a good 5 V / 2 A USB
adapter connected to the NodeMCU micro-USB socket.

For the final installation, the better arrangement is to split the regulated
5 V supply directly:

  5 V supply +  -> NodeMCU VIN or micro-USB 5 V
  5 V supply +  -> PCM5102 VIN
  5 V supply +  -> PAM8403 POWER +

  5 V supply -  -> NodeMCU G
  5 V supply -  -> PCM5102 GND
  5 V supply -  -> PAM8403 POWER -

Do not connect two different 5 V power supplies at the same time.


PART D - 1000 uF / 10 V CAPACITOR
---------------------------------

Capacitor lead           Connect to PAM8403
----------------------   -------------------------
Positive lead (+)        POWER +
Negative lead (-)        POWER -

The negative side is identified by the stripe containing minus (-) symbols on
the capacitor body. Because the leads may have been cut, do not identify
polarity only by which lead is longer.

Install this capacitor close to the PAM8403 power holes.

Never reverse this capacitor. A reversed electrolytic capacitor can heat,
leak, or burst.


PART E - ONE SPEAKER
--------------------

Use only the two PAM8403 holes printed as the LOUT output pair.

Speaker wire 1           PAM8403 LOUT +
Speaker wire 2           PAM8403 LOUT -

With one speaker, reversing these two speaker wires changes only acoustic
polarity; it will not stop the speaker from working.

Never connect either LOUT speaker wire to GND.

Leave both PAM8403 ROUT output holes empty.

If the sound-box cable has four loose wires, do not join all four. With power
disconnected, use a multimeter in resistance mode to identify the pair that
measures approximately 2.7 ohms. Use only that matched pair for the first test.

If the speaker LABEL says 4 ohms but the multimeter reads approximately
2.7 ohms, it is normally a 4-ohm nominal speaker. If the label itself says
2.7 or 3 ohms, keep the volume low and preferably place a 1 to 1.5 ohm,
2-watt resistor in series with one speaker wire.


PART F - IR RECEIVER - WAIT FOR IDENTIFICATION
----------------------------------------------

The photograph shows a bare three-pin IR receiver, but no readable model
number or pin names. VS1838B, TSOP, TL1838 and similar-looking receivers do not
all use the same left-to-right pin order.

The final functions will be:

IR receiver function     NodeMCU connection
----------------------   -------------------------
SIGNAL / OUT             D5 (GPIO14)
GND                      G
VCC                      3V (3.3 V)

DO NOT choose which physical IR leg is SIGNAL, GND or VCC yet.

Before connecting it, provide one sharp close-up photograph of:

  - the flat face of the IR receiver showing any printed letters/numbers, and
  - the opposite face, with the three legs separated and visible.

Connecting VCC and GND to the wrong legs can permanently damage the receiver.

-----------------------

[ ] NodeMCU RX       -> PCM5102 DIN

[ ] NodeMCU G        -> PCM5102 FLT

[ ] PCM5102 AGND     -> PAM8403 input G

[ ] Speaker wire 2   -> PAM8403 LOUT -

[ ] PAM8403 input R is empty
[ ] PCM5102 A3V3/3V3 is empty
[ ] IR receiver is not connected until its pin order is confirmed


FIRST POWER-ON TEST
-------------------

1. Turn the PAM8403 volume knob fully counter-clockwise.

2. Inspect every wire using the checklist above.

3. Make sure no loose wire strands touch the next hole.

4. Connect the USB cable to a 5 V / 2 A adapter.

5. Wait for the NodeMCU to boot and connect to Wi-Fi.

6. Slowly raise the PAM8403 volume.

7. If the NodeMCU restarts, the amplifier becomes hot, or the sound is badly
   distorted, disconnect power immediately and recheck power and speaker load.
